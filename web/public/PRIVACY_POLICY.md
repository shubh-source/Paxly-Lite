# Privacy Policy for Paxly (Relationship OS)

**Effective Date:** October 13, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 13, 2026
**Version:** 2.4.0

Welcome to **Paxly** ("Paxly", "we", "our", or "us"), operated as a dedicated Relationship Operating System designed for privacy, emotional intimacy, and trusted partnership. Your intimate digital world belongs exclusively to you and your partner. Privacy is not merely an optional feature in Paxly—it is the foundational architecture upon which every capability is engineered.

This Privacy Policy explains how Paxly collects, protects, processes, and respects your personal information when you use the Paxly mobile applications, web platforms, and associated services (collectively, the "Service").

---

## 1. Regulatory Compliance Framework

Paxly is committed to global standards of data dignity and legal compliance, including:
* **The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India)**: Ensuring lawful, transparent processing, explicit consent protocols, and accessible grievance redressal.
* **The Information Technology Act, 2000 & Reasonable Security Practices Rules (India)**.
* **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR)**: Honoring the principles of data minimization, lawful processing, purpose limitation, and comprehensive Data Subject Rights.
* **California Consumer Privacy Act & California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA / CPRA)**: Providing transparency, opt-outs, and zero sale of personal information.

---

## 2. Fundamental Architectural Privacy Principles

1. **Zero Ad Targeting & Zero Data Brokering**: We do **not** sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data, intimate memories, emotional logs, or chat transcripts to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or commercial syndicates.
2. **End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)**: Direct chat messages, voice notes, and private photos in your sanctuary are protected by client-side cryptographic keys derived on your device. Paxly servers cannot read or decrypt the contents of your end-to-end encrypted communications.
3. **Dual-Consent Relationship Sovereignty**: Intimate spaces require mutual trust. Features involving permanent media export or shared relationship closures adhere to clear dual-consent boundaries so neither partner is compromised.

## 3. Information We Collect and How We Use It

### 3.1. Account & Identity Information
* **Registration Details**: Name, email address, password (securely hashed via salted bcrypt), and generated public cryptographic keys.
* **Purpose**: To authenticate your account, secure your sessions, and allow verification during partner pairing.

### 3.2. Couple Space & Intimate Communications
* **Encrypted Chat & Voice Notes**: Encrypted payloads transmitted over secure TLS 1.3 channels and stored encrypted at rest.
* **Once-View & Ephemeral Media**: Self-destructing photos and messages that automatically expire and are purged once the designated view limit is reached.
* **Memory Vault & Love Notes**: Securely saved scrapbook photos, audio memories, and scheduled time-capsule letters that unlock on dates set by you.

### 3.3. Important Dates & Milestone Intelligence
* **Anniversary & Date Tracking**: Dates, custom occasions, titles, and notes you add to never miss important relationship moments.
* **Notifications & Countdowns**: Local and in-app countdown notifications scheduled on your devices (e.g., weekly reminders at 30 days, daily reminders during the final 7 days).

### 3.4. Mood Sync & Emotional State Logs
* Daily mood entries, emotional check-ins, and shared resonance status used solely to reflect your emotional wellness back to you and your connected partner.

### 3.5. Aura AI Assistant & Safety Engine
Paxly integrates **Aura AI** to assist with relationship reflection, communication advice, and emotional support.

* **Explicit, User-Initiated Interaction**: Aura interacts with you in dedicated AI chat sessions and lab counseling reviews only when you ask or initiate a session.
* **No Covert Eavesdropping**: Aura does **not** secretly scan or broadcast your private conversations for advertising or profiling.
* **High-Risk Crisis Safety Protocol (Special Disclosure)**:
  * In the critical event that Aura detects an imminent self-harm or suicidal crisis signal (`RISK_4`) within an AI interaction, the platform is designed to provide immediate empathetic support, crisis helpline resources, and—where configured—a **generic, non-intrusive safety notification** to your currently linked partner.
  * **Strict Privacy Guarantees during Escalation**:
    1. **Zero Transcript Disclosure**: Partner safety alerts *never* include message contents, chat quotes, transcripts, or confidential prompts.
    2. **Generic Notice Only**: The notification solely communicates that emotional check-in support is recommended (e.g., *"Aura noticed your partner may need emotional care"*).
    3. **Verified Recipient Only**: Safety alerts are dispatched *exclusively* to your server-verified, currently linked partner. They are *never* sent to arbitrary contacts, address books, or external parties.
    4. **User Preference Modes**: Users can adjust Aura's safety mode in Settings between `Ask first` (consent prompt before notifying), `Pre-authorized`, or `Off` (which suppresses all outbound safety alerts).
    5. **Duplicate Suppression**: A strict 15-minute atomic cooldown prevents repetitive or runaway notifications.

### 3.6. Security Logs & Intruder Detection
* To protect your account against physical snooping, Paxly offers PIN protection, stealth calculator mode, and optional intruder logs (recording failed PIN attempt timestamps and optional selfie captures stored locally on your device).

### 3.7. Payment & Subscription Data
* Payments for Love Shop items, virtual tokens, or Premium Aura memberships are processed securely through certified PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (such as Razorpay or Stripe). Paxly does **not** store or have access to your full credit card or debit card numbers.

## 4. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only under the following limited circumstances:
* **With Your Linked Partner**: Your shared messages, approved vault media, mood check-ins, and anniversary dates are shared with the partner you deliberately link via a mutual pairing code.
* **Service Infrastructure Providers**: Trusted cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., database hosting and secure storage) acting strictly under data processing agreements that mandate confidentiality and security.
* **Legal Compliance & Imminent Harm Prevention**: If compelled by a valid court order, subpoena, or applicable statutory law, or to prevent imminent physical harm as permitted by relevant data protection legislation.

We do **not** sell or trade your data to third parties under any circumstances.

---

## 5. Your Rights as a Data Principal / User

Regardless of your geographic location, Paxly provides you with full control over your personal data:

1. **Right to Access & Portability**: You can inspect your profile and export your joint history and scrapbook data in machine-readable JSON format directly from the **Settings > Export Memories** menu.
2. **Right to Rectification**: You can update your profile name, password, security questions, or anniversary dates at any time.
3. **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)**: You can request permanent account deletion. Upon account deletion, personal identifiers, chat history, and media are completely purged from active systems within 30 days.
4. **Right to Revoke Consent**: You can unlink from a partner, toggle Aura AI features off, or disable notifications whenever you choose.
5. **Right to Nominate / Grievance Redressal**: Under Indian DPDP Act provisions, you have the right to nominate an individual to exercise your rights in the event of incapacitation, and the right to swift grievance resolution.

## 6. Data Security & Storage

* **Encryption in Transit & at Rest**: All server communications use TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. Databases and storage volumes are encrypted with AES-256 standard encryption.
* **Screen Privacy & Screenshot Defense**: The application integrates native privacy-screen shields to prevent unintended recording, app-switcher previews, or unauthorized screen grabs of sensitive interactions.
* **Data Localization**: In accordance with regional guidelines, user data is routed and hosted in secure tier-3/4 data centers adhering to SOC2, ISO 27001, and GDPR compliance standards.

---

## 7. Retention and Account Closure

* **Active Accounts**: We retain your shared memories and dates so long as your account remains active, ensuring your relationship history is preserved.
* **Closure Request**: If partners decide to part ways, our **Account Closure** flow allows freeing your user ID while keeping past shared scrapbooks archived in an inaccessible vault state according to mutual consent, or scheduling full deletion.
* **Inactivity & Backups**: Secure database backups are cycled and permanently purged according to rolling 30-day retention schedules.

## 8. Children and Minors

Paxly is strictly built for **consenting adults aged 18 years and older** (or the age of legal majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors. If we learn that an individual under 18 has created an account, the account and associated data will be promptly terminated.
