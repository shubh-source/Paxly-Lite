# Privacy Policy for Vlynxly

**Effective Date:** May 6, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 6, 2026

Welcome to Vlynxly ("we," "our," or "us"). Vlynxly is a Relationship Operating System designed for ultimate privacy and emotional intimacy. Your privacy is not a feature; it is our foundation.

This Privacy Policy complies with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023 (India)**, **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU)**, and **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)**.

---

## 1. Data Collection & Usage

### 1.1. End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)
All personal communications, including Text Messages, Voice Whispers, and Media shared in the Chat, are End-to-End Encrypted. Vlynxly does not have the keys to decrypt or read your private messages.

### 1.2. Aura AI Analysis
Aura AI does **not** monitor your private conversations in the background. AI analysis is only performed when a user provides an **explicit, one-time grant** (e.g., selecting a 7, 30, or 90-day analysis period). Once the analysis is complete, the temporary access is revoked.

### 1.3. The Vault & Media
Media stored in "The Vault" is encrypted and only accessible by you and your linked partner. Under our **Dual-Consent Model**, no media can be downloaded to a local device without the explicit real-time permission of the partner.

---

## 2. Your Rights (Data Principal Rights)

According to Indian and International laws, you have the following rights:
*   **Right to Access:** You can view all data we hold about you at any time.
*   **Right to Correction:** You can update your profile and relationship details.
*   **Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten):** You can delete your account. Upon deletion, all associated data (Chat, Vault, Voice Notes) is permanently purged from our servers within 30 days.
*   **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You can revoke AI access or partner links at any time.

---

## 3. Security Measures

*   **Screenshot Protection:** We implement active detection of screen capture attempts. Any attempt to record or screenshot private calls results in immediate session termination.
*   **Zero Third-Party Sharing:** We do **not** sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third-party advertisers or data brokers.
*   **Data Localization:** For Indian users, data is processed and stored in compliance with Indian data localization guidelines.
